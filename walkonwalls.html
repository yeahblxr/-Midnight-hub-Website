Walk on Walls.

Walk on walls in any game. Reset your character after executing to get started. No key needed. Copy the loader below and run it in your executor.

walk-on-walls.lua 
loadstring(game:HttpGet("https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yeahblxr/-Midnight-hub/refs/heads/main/Midnight%20wallwalking"))()

Running it takes three steps

  1. 1

    Copy the script

    Press Copy script. The full loader goes to your clipboard.

  2. 2

    Paste into your executor

    The script is keyless, so there is nothing to edit before you run it.

  3. 3

    Execute

    Join the game and press Execute, then reset your character to start walking on walls.
